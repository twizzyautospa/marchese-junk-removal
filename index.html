FAQ

Good questions.Straight answers.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on volume: how much space your items take up in the trailer. We look at the pile, give you the exact price and only start once you say yes.

Do I have to bring anything to the curb?

No. We carry items out from wherever they are: the garage, the basement, the backyard, upstairs. Just show us what's going.

How soon can you come?

Tell us your timeline in the form. If you need it gone fast, pick "As soon as possible" and we'll offer the earliest opening we have, including same-day when the schedule allows.

What kinds of things do you take?

Furniture, mattresses, appliances, renovation debris, yard waste, electronics, hot tubs, sheds and general household or office clutter. If you're not sure about an item, add it to the notes and we'll confirm.

Do you clean up afterwards?

Yes. Once everything is loaded we sweep the area so the space is ready to use.

Is a junk pickup better than renting a bin?

For most home jobs, yes. There's no steel bin sitting on your driveway, no permit and no loading it yourself. We arrive, load and leave in one visit.
